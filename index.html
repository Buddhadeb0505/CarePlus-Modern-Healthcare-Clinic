ABOUT CAREPLUS

Healthcare should feel human.

CarePlus Modern Healthcare Clinic is a concept for a welcoming, patient-focused clinic in Kolkata, West Bengal. Our goal is to make it easier for people to explore care options, connect with healthcare professionals and take the next step with confidence.

We bring clear information, thoughtful communication and a convenient digital experience together—so patients and families know where to begin.
